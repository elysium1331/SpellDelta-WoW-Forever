## What the addon is

[Elysium] SpellDelta is a World of Warcraft addon. Hold Alt over an item or a talent and
its tooltip shows what that item or talent changes about the player's spells: each spell's
number before and after, and the gain (for example "Renew 211 to 231 (+20)"). The idea in
one picture: **a spell growing stronger, before and after.**

It belongs to the same addon collection as the attached image (ProfessionWheel's logo).
**Match the attached image's style:** a round medallion seen face-on, hammered-gold rim,
dark near-black and warm brown disc (#17140f, rim #9e8552), painterly but clean fantasy
rendering, soft top-left light, a warm glow at the center, small four-point gold sparkles,
deep warm brown-black vignette background (not transparent).
